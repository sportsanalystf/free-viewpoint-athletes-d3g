Each line is one Gaussian over 150 frames, seen from a camera the model never trained on. The director uses exactly these tracks to find the key moment and the motion direction.

Q3 asks whether the model captures how the athletes actually move. Because every Gaussian keeps its identity over time, each one leaves a 3D track. Here are 700 of them drawn into a held-out camera. In basketball you can see the passes, in football the arc of the throw, and in softball the loop of the bat. These are the same tracks the director uses to find the key moment and decide where to look.
