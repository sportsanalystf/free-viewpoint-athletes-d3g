How the director got better

v1

Smooth, but too close to the players.

v2

Stray floaters counted as players, so the camera backed into the wall.

v3

The floater filter was too strict and dropped the second player.

v6

Density filter, clearance, occlusion lift, auto lens. Follows the play.

This slide is the same moment from four versions of the director, and it shows what each fix was for. Version one was smooth but sat on top of the players. In version two, stray floaters were treated as players, and the clearance rule pushed the camera into the dome wall, which is why that frame is just colour. Version three filtered too hard and lost the second player. Version six keeps dense clusters only, keeps its distance, lifts over blockers and adjusts the lens, and follows the play.
