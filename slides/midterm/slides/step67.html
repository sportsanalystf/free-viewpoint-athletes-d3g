Track every frame after that

WHAT IT DOES

Each new frame starts from the last and gets 2k iterations. Only position and rotation change. Neighbours must move rigidly, rotate together and keep their distance.

WHY

Fixed colour and size keep each Gaussian the same “piece” of the athlete. The priors stop pieces drifting apart.

WHAT WE GOT

A 4D model over 150 frames, with a 3D track for every point.

Paths of 700 moving Gaussians over 150 frames, seen from a held-out camera

Steps six and seven track the rest of the clip. Each frame starts from the previous one and gets two thousand iterations. Colour, size and opacity are frozen; only position and rotation change. On top of the image loss, neighbouring Gaussians are asked to move like a rigid piece, rotate together and keep their distances.
