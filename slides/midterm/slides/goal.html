Goal

Synchronized multi-angle video in, an immersive cinematic 3D replay out

INPUT

31 synced, calibrated cameras

4D MODEL

Gaussians that move over time

OUTPUT

Free viewpoint, slow motion, freeze-time

One use is sports broadcasting. Others: coaching, analysis, AR/VR. And we measure how faithful it is.

So the goal is simple to say: take synchronized video from many angles and produce an immersive, cinematic 3D replay. In the middle sits a 4D model, a cloud of Gaussians that move over time. Broadcasting is the obvious use, but coaching and AR or VR work the same way. And unlike the closed systems, we measure how faithful the result is.
