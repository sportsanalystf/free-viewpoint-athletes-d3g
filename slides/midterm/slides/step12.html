A · Prepare the data · steps 1–2

Frames, calibration and masks

WHAT IT DOES

Loads every frame with its camera intrinsics and pose, plus a mask of the athlete.

WHY

Rendering into a camera needs exact cameras. The mask teaches each Gaussian whether it is athlete or background.

WHAT WE GOT

31 calibrated views per frame and a clean athlete mask for each.

Image, mask and overlay for three cameras

Step one and two. We load every frame with its camera’s intrinsics and pose; rendering a Gaussian into a camera only works if these are exact. We also load a foreground mask per image. D3G uses the mask to give each Gaussian an athlete-or-background label, and later the director uses that label to find the players.
