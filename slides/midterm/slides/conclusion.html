Conclusion

What we have so far

01

An open pipeline from synchronized multi-angle video to a cinematic 3D replay, tested step by step.

02

Faithful 4D models: 27–29 dB on unseen cameras across all six sports, stable over time, with real 3D motion tracks.

03

A director that works across sports: six clips, no tuning, about 35× smoother than blending real cameras.

Limitations: five of six replays use released models; fast, thin objects blur; some opening shots start behind the athlete; a lab dome is smaller and better lit than a stadium.

To wrap up. We have an open pipeline from synchronized multi-angle video to a cinematic 3D replay, and we checked every step along the way. The 4D models match unseen cameras well on all six sports, stay stable over the clip, and track real 3D motion. The director turns them into smooth replays without per-clip tuning. The honest limitations: released models for five of the replays, blur on fast thin objects, a few opening shots from behind, and a lab dome isn’t a stadium.
