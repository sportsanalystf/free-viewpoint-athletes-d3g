D · Direct the replay · steps 11–12

Plan the shot, keep it clean

WHAT IT DOES

Looks across the main motion so a throw or swing is seen side-on. Flies a push-in, a 5 s freeze-time sweep of 130°, a crane and a settle. Stays at least 0.35 dome radii from every player, rises if someone blocks the view, widens the lens to fit the play.

WHY

That’s how a broadcast camera operator would shoot it, and it never depends on where the real cameras are.

Steps eleven and twelve plan the shot. The director finds the main direction of the fast motion and looks across it, so a throw or swing is seen from the side. Then it flies a fixed shot list: a push-in, a five-second freeze-time sweep of 130 degrees, a crane and a settle. While doing that it stays clear of every player, rises if someone blocks the view, and widens the lens to keep the play in frame. The map shows the basketball path: smooth, around both players, and independent of where the real cameras are.
