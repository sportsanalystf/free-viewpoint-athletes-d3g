The catch

None of it is open, and none of it is measured

FIRST TRY

One broadcast camera

Tracked players, fit 3D bodies and splats on an MLB at-bat and a college game.

PROBLEM

Looked fine, can’t check

No second camera saw the scene, so there was nothing to compare against.

SO WE SWITCHED

Synced multi-view

Many calibrated cameras, with some held back to grade the result.

The catch is that none of these systems share their full pipeline or report accuracy. I first tried the single-camera route: tracking players, fitting 3D bodies and splats on an MLB at-bat and a college basketball game. The new views looked okay, but I had no way to check them, because no other camera saw the scene. So I switched to synchronized multi-view video, where I can hold some cameras back and compare my renders with real images.
