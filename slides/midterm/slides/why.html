Blending real camera poses copies the rig’s uneven spacing. Planning around the players gives a smooth path wherever the cameras are.

Why should this work? First, Dynamic 3D Gaussians keeps each Gaussian’s identity through the whole clip. That gives a 3D track for every point, which the director uses to find motion, and it lets us render between frames for slow motion. Second, it’s the same recipe Arcturus describes publicly, so an open version done carefully should get close in a controlled dome. Third, where we expect to beat the simple baseline is the camera move: blending real camera poses copies the rig’s uneven spacing, while planning around the players gives a smooth path no matter where the cameras are.
