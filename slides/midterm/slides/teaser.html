The result first

Six sports, one automatic replay director

Slow motion, a freeze-time sweep and a crane move, planned automatically for each clip. No real camera stood at any of these viewpoints.

Let me show the result first. These are six different sports from the same pipeline. For each clip, the system found the key moment, picked a side-on angle, and flew a push-in, a freeze-time sweep and a crane. None of these angles had a real camera. The rest of the talk explains how we get from raw camera footage to this.
