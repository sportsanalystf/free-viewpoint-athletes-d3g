Some background from class. NeRF stores a scene in a network and renders beautiful images, but slowly. 3D Gaussian Splatting replaced the network with millions of small blobs that render in real time, but only for still scenes. Dynamic 3D Gaussians, from 3DV 2024, keeps the same blobs for the whole clip and moves them frame by frame, so it also tracks every point. That’s our base. Other papers we read connect too: D4RT and CoTracker3 recover motion, VGGT and MapAnything could give a starting point cloud from few cameras, and RayZer and WildRayZer learn view synthesis without 3D labels.
