All four are closed: no full pipeline to study, and no accuracy numbers. Screenshots taken Nov 2026.

This started in class, when we saw World Labs’ Atlas, which can take real video and reframe it from new camera angles. Then I found Arcturus, which captures real sports action in full 3D for broadcasters. On social media, a computer-vision engineer at Roboflow rebuilt an NBA play in 3D from a single broadcast camera, and another person posted a Gaussian splat of a World Series home run right next to the real broadcast view. None of these share a full pipeline or report accuracy, so I wanted to build an open version myself.
