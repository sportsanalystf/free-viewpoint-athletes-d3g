Here’s the same moment from each sport’s replay. Basketball works best: it freezes at the ball’s peak and the aim follows the ball from player to player. Boxes is a slow movement and gives the calmest replay. Football and juggling look good during the freeze, but the opening shot starts behind the athlete, which a camera operator wouldn’t do. Softball and tennis are seen side-on during the swing, but thin objects like the bat and racket blur when they move fast. One more honest note: the ball detector looks for red Gaussians, and in boxes, which has no ball, it locked onto red wrapping paper.
